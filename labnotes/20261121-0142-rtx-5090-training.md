# RTX 5090 training run

## Provisioning and review

- Rented one verified RTX 5090 in a South Korea datacenter at $0.5304/hour
  including 200 GiB. The offer listed 16 effective CPU cores and 607 Mbps
  download speed. No local dataset downloads were made.
- Initial bootstrap reached SSH and installed system prerequisites and uv.
  The parent shell then failed to find uv after the child setup exited.
  Added the local bin directory to remote command, bootstrap and job PATHs.
  Restarted the existing rental's bootstrap as a `vxpipe` job.
- GPT-6 Astra reviewed the pipeline. Addressed elapsed pause audio, integer
  PCM overflow during babble, corpus language/TSV formats, datacenter
  filtering and decision-time coverage. The rental was verified against
  the datacenter and price filters before it was created.
- Added deterministic language quotas within sources and kept speaker groups
  intact across languages. The benchmark groups canonical language codes
  across sources. Added quarter-point notifications for long steps and asked
  GPT-6 Luna to monitor the training run and watcher.

## Real-data smoke

- Recovery bootstrap passed in 1m05s and pulled the project storage prefix.
  The first smoke download transferred 951.89 MB in 1m55s, about 8.27 MB/s.
  The first pipeline stopped after 3m42s at Emilia-YODAS. All 100 inspected
  metadata files used the `_id` key, while the adapter expected `id`.
  The fix handles both schemas. The retry reused 949.75 MB of planned data
  and completed in 2m27s. Its bootstrap and smoke jobs both exited 0.
- The smoke speaker/label counts were AMI English 3/17, Common Voice Telugu
  4/62, Emilia-YODAS German 23/207, FLEURS Hindi 0 known IDs/260, Indic
  DiarBench Kashmiri 9/214 and VoxPopuli Slovenian 12/1,028. MUSAN's first
  inspected 50 MB contained music. The full acquisition will show whether
  its byte cap reaches the noise subdirectory.
- Smoke wrote 937 training, 192 validation and 659 test pause examples.
  The three test conditions yielded 3,106 shard copies across the run.
  Three GPU training steps ran with the Python 3.12 campaign stack. The
  recurrent model had 144,975 parameters, a 300,447-byte int8 export and
  float parity error of 2.98e-8. CPU p99 was 0.89 ms on 25 repetitions
  for the 1 s smoke model. Quality gates remain open.
- Launched the full acquisition as the `download` job. Checkpoint B's
  remaining gates cover the full selection, label and shard inventory, R2
  verification and listening review.

## Review and training preflight

- Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct was used on the rented machine as an independent
  audit probe. Its 16.81 GB of auditor weights finished downloading in
  3m48s. The pinned model's Apache 2.0 weights were used for review only.
  The constrained follow-up supported 17 of 40 manual-reference
  calibration examples, contradicted 18 and left five unresolved.
  It supported 16 of the 50 fixed per-track samples and was biased toward
  shift. Neither audio-language configuration passed calibration.
  [Auditor model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct)
- The direct word-timing and human dialogue-act inventory supported
  989 of the 7,665 AMI labels. At 300 ms, the supported subset has 789
  train pauses from 132 speakers, 104 validation pauses from 12, and 59
  test pauses from 10. The test subset has 28 holds and 31 shifts. Most
  other labels remain unresolved by this automatic check. Requested the
  user's approval to use the smaller, reference-supported review scope.
- The validation-only pilot ran 256 GPU steps in 39s including validation.
  The observed training rate was about nine steps/s. Its validation AUC
  was 0.5775, and recall was 7.33% at 5.00% false completes. It is a pace
  probe and no decision used the test split.
- Generated-audio augmentation ran at about 110 examples/s with one
  worker and 619/s with eight. In-process PyAV reduced the measured
  Opus call from 61 to 22 ms and pitch from 31 to under 1 ms.
  A generated forward/backward probe used 64 examples per step and
  peaked below 1 GB allocation on the 8 s, near-1M candidate.

## AMI timing calibration

- Across 7,665 AMI labels, 989 had matching aligned word timing and speaker
  order. Of the 878 contradictions, 819 had annotated speech inside the
  early pause and 59 disagreed on which speaker continued. The 5,798
  unresolved cases include 5,339 without a nearby caller word end, 180
  outside the next-onset tolerance, 274 ambiguous short replies and five
  unclear continuations after a backchannel. See `reports/ami-audit-reasons.json`.
- The `ami-timing` job compared 6,512 training labels. Of the 4,551
  pairs within 600 ms on both boundaries, median absolute differences
  were 252.8 ms at caller speech end and 49.6 ms at next speech onset.
  The corresponding 90th percentiles were 494 and 130 ms. Signed medians
  were -44 and -48 ms. See `reports/ami-timing.json`.
- With 200/250 ms tolerances, 817 training labels were supported and 754
  contradicted. At 300/375 ms the counts were 862 and 1,584, and at
  500/625 ms they were 917 and 3,073. The checks for speech in the early
  pause and speaker order ran throughout. No validation or test examples
  informed the timing sweep.

## Approved training scope

- The user approved training with verified AMI examples and retaining the
  original validation and frozen test splits. Of the 862 supported training
  labels, 532 met the original speaker caps and were present in the shards.
  These comprise 262 holds and 270 shifts from 132 speakers. The filter
  applies both to primary training examples and the AMI augmentation voice
  pool. See `reports/ami-verified-training.json`. The 90% review target was
  unmet and the original evaluation labels carry that limitation.
- The full five-epoch staged experiment queue started in `vxpipe` with
  `configs/experiments.json`. Recovery fine-tunes failed int8 candidates
  with simulated weight and linear input quantization, then rechecks the
  deployed ONNX artifact against the original float AUC on validation.
  Both generated recovery checkpoints trained on the RTX 5090 and exported.
  The campaign suite passed 48 tests before adding the AMI selection fixture.
- The separate raw R2 push completed in 57m00s for 17.06 GB. The original
  estimate rose as transfer speed slowed near the end. Read-back checks
  found 138,742 matching raw files and nine matching manifests, with zero
  differences in either check. The backup job exited successfully.
- Astra reproduced a timing edge case in which a verified label allowed a
  training query after the annotated resumption. The onset limit excludes
  10 of the 532 candidates that resume before 200 ms from the Silero edge
  and removes at least one query from 42 of the other 522. Final counts
  are 260 holds and 262 shifts from 132 speakers. The eligibility list and report record shard,
  label, annotation and verifier fingerprints. Babble voices also respect
  each run's source and fraction filters.
- Intentionally stopped the original `experiments` job after 4m53s
  (exit 130) during the first run. No checkpoint was complete.
  Started `experiments-verified` with the corrected filter and unchanged
  frozen evaluation splits. Initial first-run pace was about eight steps
  per second, with a roughly 28-minute training estimate.
- Validation, quality benchmarks and volume sweeps score independent
  batch-one ONNX calls. Int8 validation ranks the three eligible exports
  before evaluating test. Decision percentiles include censored shifts,
  and a silence-only comparison requires at least the baseline recall.
  The updated RTX 5090 suite passed all 51 tests in 34.38s.

## Evaluation label evidence

- The full-data configurations have 177,416 unique training examples
  after using 522 of the 4,054 AMI training shards. The frozen sets have
  42,847 validation and 72,949 test examples.
- The original AMI test labels have 64 supported, 47 contradicted and
  388 unresolved examples. Silero supplies proposed acoustic pause times
  and the labeller uses the next speaker to infer hold or shift. The
  independent check uses aligned word timestamps and human dialogue acts.
  AMI documented that word timings were derived by automatically aligning
  the human transcript to the audio. The user confirmed AMI as the reference
  for the English meeting examples. [AMI transcription](https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/transcription.shtml)
- The original word-timing check supported 59 examples whose Silero gap
  reaches 300 ms. Four resume earlier according to AMI's annotations, so
  the diagnostic scope keeps 55 examples, 28 holds and 27 shifts from
  10 speakers. `superturn benchmark-ami-reference --prepare` froze that
  scope in `reports/ami-reference-test-scope.json` before model scoring.
  `superturn benchmark-ami-reference` will report the selected models'
  performance at their validation thresholds on the three frozen
  conditions. The reference check allows for timing differences around
  the initial Silero edge. The full set remains the primary benchmark.
- Replay of the scope preparation reproduced its membership, two
  generated end-to-end export tests passed, and the scope was copied to R2.
- The first run (`s1-a-150000-17`) finished epoch 1. Validation at
  300 ms had 31,836 holds, 8,092 shifts, 4.997% false completes, 46.07%
  complete recall and ROC AUC 0.9102. This is an intermediate epoch at a
  validation-selected threshold of 0.17235, before any frozen test scoring.

## Training throughput

- `profile-train-input` measured 1,393 examples/s for reading and converting
  Parquet batches. With generated 3 s PCM and the same noise and room assets,
  augmentation yielded 447–496 examples/s with eight workers and 415–452
  with 16 workers while the live job ran. Retained eight workers for the
  experiment queue. The first run crossed 25% at 7m30s.
- AMI's official meeting index contains 190 global speaker IDs across 171
  meetings. The four-minute slices yielded labelled pauses from 184 voices.
  The selected AMI training rows span 132 voices at a median of four and
  a maximum of 12 pauses per voice. AMI contributes 0.29% of the
  full-data training set. The user confirmed the preference to limit AMI
  reuse because the corpus has relatively few distinct voices.

## First checkpoint conversation validation

- The first run's epoch 2 reached 60.11% recall at 4.997% false
  completes on the pooled clean validation set, with ROC AUC 0.9463.
  The checkpoint threshold was 0.29945 at a 300 ms query.
- A separate validation-only probe kept that threshold and scored the
  same 575 AMI pauses from held-out speakers in clean and G.711 telephone
  conditions. Telephone results were 47.95% false completes, 45.26%
  recall and AUC 0.456. The annotation-supported subset had 103 cases
  and measured 52.11% false completes, 40.63% recall and AUC 0.363.
  Clean AMI AUC was 0.450 on all 575 cases. Pooled validation does
  not currently predict conversational performance for this checkpoint.
  Results and the checkpoint hash are in
  `reports/ami-validation-epoch-probe.json` and were copied to R2.
- This probe uses only validation. Compare the completed stage 1
  checkpoints on AMI telephone validation before deciding how to rank
  candidates for the remaining experiments.
- `superturn compare-ami-validation` compares verified finished Stage 1
  checkpoints using the fixed AMI validation rows and G.711 transform.
  It reports the saved pooled threshold and an AMI-only diagnostic fit
  at the 5% false-complete target. Each report includes all eligible AMI
  rows and the annotation-supported subset, with no test data input.
  Two generated end-to-end tests passed on the RTX 5090 server.
- `s1-a-150000-17` completed all five epochs and 14,135 steps in
  28m21s. The early estimate was about 28 minutes. It reached 74.79%
  recall at 4.997% false completes on pooled validation, with AUC
  0.9640. The completion marker records its checkpoint SHA and
  training signature. R2 copied 1.17 MB of run artifacts. The queue
  reached 1 of 37 and started seed 29. The queue's initial remaining
  estimate is 17h06m using the first observed run, before the pace of
  larger and shorter variants is measured.
- `compare-ami-stage1` verified the complete checkpoint and scored
  575 AMI telephone validation pauses at 300 ms. Its saved pooled
  threshold yielded 12.10% false completes and 5.11% recall.
  Fitting the threshold on the same AMI validation rows yielded 4.79%
  false completes and 2.92% recall. AUC was 0.451 overall and 0.476
  on the 103 annotation-supported validation cases.
  See `reports/stage1-ami-validation.json`, also copied to R2.
- R2 read-back found six matching run files and 17 matching report files
  with zero differences. The first checkpoint was fetched locally and its
  SHA-256 matched the completion marker. The snapshot included nine valid
  source manifests and zero audio files. It occupied 217 MB of logical
  file size, of which 212 MB were label metadata. The fetch command now
  offers `--results-only` to omit labels in routine result retrieval.
- The second 150k GRU seed (`s1-a-150000-29`) completed in 28m08s
  and pushed its 587.84 kB result. Epoch 5 reached 66.93% pooled clean
  validation recall at 4.997% false completes, with AUC 0.9551.
  `compare-ami-stage1-seeds` reran the AMI telephone comparison for
  both verified checkpoints. Seed 29 had AUC 0.525 overall and 0.432 on
  the supported subset. At the diagnostic AMI-fitted threshold it
  had 5.84% recall at 4.79% false completes, compared with 2.92% recall
  for seed 17 at the same false-complete rate. The queue advanced to
  2 of 37 and started the first 500k GRU seed.
